# van Dorp Microbial Genomics Group

We are a microbial genomics research group at UCL studying the evolution,
epidemiology and population genomics of microbial pathogens from past to present.

## Research

Our work uses genomic and computational approaches to understand microbial
evolution, transmission and antimicrobial resistance.

## Software and resources

This GitHub organisation hosts software, analysis pipelines and other
resources developed by the group.
